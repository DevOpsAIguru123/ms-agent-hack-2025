# Personal Assistant

## Features

This personal assistant can help you with:

- **Bank Statements**: Search and review your financial transactions
- **Google Calendar**: Check your schedule and upcoming events  
- **Google Tasks**: Manage your to-do lists and tasks
- **Weather Information**: Get current and forecasted weather conditions
- **Web Search**: Find information on the internet

## Examples

Try asking:
- "What's the weather like today in New York?"
- "Show me my calendar for tomorrow"
- "Create a new task to buy groceries"
- "Delete my task about buying groceries"
- "Show me my tasks"
- "Find transactions from Starbucks in my bank statements"
- "Search for information about Mars"
